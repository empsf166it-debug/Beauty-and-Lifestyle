Grasse Atelier • Established 2012

"A fragrance is the most intense form of memory; an invisible yet unforgettable portrait of the human soul."

Born from a reverie of timeless elegance and botanical purity.

Founded in 2012 by a collective of master noses from Grasse and Florence, Scentora was created to restore perfumery to its golden age of slow artisanal formulation, unrestrained by mass-market formulas.

We travel to secluded corners of the globe: picking centifolia roses under the dawn mist in Provence, hand-scraping agarwood resin in Southeast Asia, and aging Florentine orris roots in cool stone cellars for over three years.

Our Fragrance Philosophy

Four uncompromising tenets that guide every drop formulated inside the Scentora atelier.

The Art of Perfumery

We view perfumery not as chemistry, but as poetic architecture. Each blend requires a minimum of 90 days of maceration and maturation in darkened temperature-regulated vaults before being hand-bottled.

Slow Maceration • Non-Industrialized

Premium Ingredients

We refuse synthetic fillers or watered down carriers. All Scentora creations are crafted at true Extrait de Parfum concentration (25% - 32% pure perfume oils), yielding exceptional silage and longevity.

Extrait Strength • Ethical Botanicals

Quality & Craftsmanship

Our heavy glass flacons are forged by master glassmakers in Normandy, polished by hand, and sealed with magnetic zinc alloy caps engraved with the Scentora seal of authenticity.

Normandy Crystal • Magnetic Caps

Signature Olfactory Experience

We believe perfume is intimate. A scent should never announce your presence before you arrive, but linger warmly as an affectionate memory long after you depart.

Subtle Presence • 24-Hour Evolution
